How buying works

No account, no email, no subscription. You pay in crypto, the payment is checked against the public chain, and the file unlocks on the page. The whole thing takes about a minute once the transaction confirms.

STEP 1

Pick the file

Four products: the $5 cheat sheet, the $19 scanner, the $12 journal, or both tools for $25. Each card has a “What’s inside” section — read it before paying, it lists every file you receive.

STEP 2

Choose a chain and send the exact amount

BNB Chain or Solana. The page shows an address, a QR code and the exact amount in BNB or SOL, and locks that quote for 20 minutes. Send only the coin and network shown — a USDT transfer or a different chain will not be credited.

STEP 3

Paste the transaction hash

After your wallet confirms, copy the transaction hash (TxID) and paste it into the box on the page. The server checks the amount, the recipient address and the chain directly — no screenshots, no “send me your receipt”, no waiting for a human.

STEP 4

Download

A download button appears on the page the moment the check passes. Save the file somewhere you will find it again; the link stays valid for this order, but there is no account to log back into.

Almost every failed purchase is one of these five. Fixes are in the table.

“Amount too low”Exchange fee was taken out of the transfer Send the shortfall to the same address and paste the new hash, or message me with both hashes.
“Amount too high”You sent more than the quote Message me — the order is honoured, the excess is refunded or credited to another file.
Quote expiredMore than 20 minutes passed Click Buy again for a fresh quote. Do not send to an expired amount.
“Not found yet”The chain has not confirmed, or the hash is truncated Wait 30–60 seconds and paste again; make sure you copied the full hash, not the wallet’s order ID.

Never send from an exchange’s internal “transfer to user” feature, and never send USDT to a BNB or SOL address. If you are not sure, message me first — a two-minute question is cheaper than a lost transfer.
