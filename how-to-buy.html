Questions

SHORT ANSWERS
Is it really free?

Yes. The four data packs cost $0 for signed-in members, and creating the account is free. There is no hidden checkout step and no payment form on the packs page.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. Wallets were only needed for the old paid checkout, which is no longer part of the download flow. You only need an email and a password.

Why is signing in required?

Downloads are tied to a member account so the files are not open to anonymous scraping, and so future revisions can be matched to the account that downloaded them. It also keeps one simple rule for all four files.

What are the four packs?

A one-page cheatsheet, an altcoin radar dataset, a trade journal template and a bundle that contains all of them. Sizes and contents are listed on the packs page.

Can I share the files?

The packs are for your own research. Please don’t redistribute or resell them; the site is run by one person and the files are the thank-you for signing up.

The download didn’t start — what now?

Check that the header shows you as signed in, then press the button again. Some browsers block several downloads in a row; allow downloads for this site and retry. If it still fails, reach out on Telegram.

What happened to the old payment instructions?

They described a checkout flow that no longer applies to these packs and have been removed from this page. Anything you already paid for keeps working as before.
