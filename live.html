Questions people ask

What does this screen for?Funding-rate extremes (crowded longs or shorts) and unusual 24-hour moves across the Bitget USDT perpetual universe. Contracts with less than $3M of 24h volume, majors and tokenised stocks are filtered out.

Is it really free?Yes. It runs entirely in your browser against public exchange endpoints — no account, no API key, no server of ours in the middle.

Is this trading advice?No. It is data: who is crowded, who is paying, what moved. Deciding what to do with it is your job.

How often does it update?Every 5 minutes automatically, or immediately if you press Refresh.

Free, browser-only data

This page reads two public Bitget endpoints directly from your browser:

api.bitget.com/api/v2/mix/market/contracts
api.bitget.com/api/v2/mix/market/tickers

No account, no API key, and no server of ours in the middle. If Bitget is unreachable, the list shows an empty state and the timestamp says the scan failed.
