Questions people ask

What does this screen for?
Funding-rate extremes (crowded longs or shorts) and unusual 24-hour moves across the Bitget USDT perpetual universe. Contracts with less than $3M of 24h volume, majors and tokenised stocks are filtered out.

Is it really free?
Yes. It runs entirely in your browser against public exchange endpoints — no account, no API key, no server of ours in the middle.

Is this trading advice?
No. It is data: who is crowded, who is paying, what moved. Deciding what to do with it is your job.

How often does it update?
Every 5 minutes automatically, or immediately if you press Refresh.
