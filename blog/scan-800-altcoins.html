How I scan 800+ altcoin perpetuals in 30 seconds

Every screener I tried had the same problem: it was either a paid dashboard with a fixed watchlist, or a spreadsheet I had to maintain. Neither answers the question I actually have at 9am — what changed overnight that I should look at?

The data is free — the filtering is the product

Every major exchange exposes public market endpoints. Bitget's USDT-perpetual universe is around 800 contracts, and one call returns you all of them:

GET https://api.bitget.com/api/v2/mix/market/tickers?productType=USDT-FUTURES

Each row has what you need: last price, 24h change, quote volume, funding rate and open interest (as holdingAmount). No key, no account, ~200KB of JSON.

So the raw data is not the moat. The moat is the filter. A screener that shows you 800 coins is the same as no screener.

Three filters that remove 99% of the noise

1. Volume floor. Anything under a few million dollars of 24h volume has a fake-looking order book; a $5k order moves it 20%. Skip it before you look at any signal.

2. Move and funding thresholds, not rankings. "Top gainers" is a popularity contest. A coin is interesting when its funding is far from normal (the crowd is crowded) or its 24h move is unusual (something happened), not when it is first in a list sorted by the same number everyone else is looking at.

3. Market beta. Compare each coin's 24h move to ETH's. If ETH is down 5% and the coin is down 5%, that is the market, not the coin. If ETH is flat and the coin is down 5%, that is the coin's own problem.

Filtering by "what happened to this coin specifically" is the difference between a screener and a signal.

The 30-second part

The whole scan is: fetch contracts (for funding intervals and to skip tokenised stocks), fetch tickers, loop once, sort by a score, print ten rows. No database, no cron, no framework — 150 lines of standard library Python.

python radar_lite.py --top 10

The free lite version is on GitHub and prints funding extremes plus 24h moves: fuba-agent/altcoin-anomaly-radar.

What the lite version doesn't do

Open interest deltas (is the move backed by new money?), 5-minute spikes, and the coin-vs-ETH comparison need either a second snapshot or a per-row candle fetch — which is where a single script becomes a small tool. That's the paid version.
